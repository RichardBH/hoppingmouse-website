LAYER 01 / FIELD

Field: sensors, drives, valves, instrumentation

Automation starts where the product moves. We specify and integrate the field layer — temperature, level, pressure and flow instrumentation, variable-speed drives, valves and actuators — into a control design that can actually be commissioned, maintained and fault-found at 3am. Condition monitoring too: bearing vibration, silo temperature and humidity, the signals that tell you a breakdown is coming.

4–20mA · HART · IO-Link · Modbus · Profinet · EtherNet/IP

LAYER 02 / CONTROL

Control: PLC and RTU engineering

Machine-level control is where reliability is won or lost. We design and program PLCs and RTUs with explicit interlocks, honest fault handling and sequencing an electrician can follow — on Siemens, Rockwell / Allen-Bradley, Schneider and IDEC platforms, plus telemetry RTUs (Kingfisher, SCADAPack) for wide-area networks. Greenfield builds, and live brownfield upgrades where production can't stop while the control system changes underneath it.

Siemens · Rockwell / Allen-Bradley · Schneider · IDEC · Kingfisher · SCADAPack · DNP3

LAYER 03 / SUPERVISION

SCADA: the operator’s single source of truth

A SCADA system earns trust by showing measured reality — run feedback, not commanded intent — and by alarming only when something needs a human. We build on Ignition, ClearSCADA / GeoSCADA and Citect, applying high-performance HMI principles: state at a glance, alarm rationalisation, trends where operators need them. We are certified agents for Qualitiv cloud SCADA — real-time monitoring, alarming and remote control with batch traceability for smaller plants that want big-plant visibility without big-plant infrastructure.

Ignition · ClearSCADA / GeoSCADA · Citect · Wonderware · Qualitiv cloud SCADA (certified agents)

LAYER 04 / INTELLIGENCE

Intelligence: alarming, traceability, MES and applied AI

Above the control layer sits the software that turns a running plant into a managed one: alarm systems that operators respect, batch and quality traceability, MES integration, and applied AI — machine vision for grading and inspection (with our partner Corematic), prediction on real process data. Every intelligence feature we deliver is tied to a measured process variable and a person who acts on it.

MES · batch traceability · machine vision · applied AI on process data

LAYER 05 / ENTERPRISE

Enterprise & cloud: data that earns its keep

Industry 4.0, delivered plainly: historians and data pipelines that move plant data into the cloud and business systems with its context intact — so production, quality and maintenance decisions are made on evidence. Secure by design: segmented OT networks, controlled remote access, and an ICS security posture informed by formal training (ICS-CERT) and utility-grade OT governance experience.

Historians · OPC UA · MQTT · cloud pipelines · OT network security
