## Situation

A large nut-processing plant in regional Victoria — one of the biggest of its
kind in the country — needed its processing line automated as one coherent
system: receiving, cleaning, drying, grading and storage, all supervised from
a single operator seat.

## Constraints

Food processing is seasonal and unforgiving. Harvest doesn't wait for
commissioning windows, drying is a 24-hour process where a control fault
costs product, and the plant had to be operable by a small site team — not a
control-room full of specialists.

## Scope owned

The full control stack was designed and delivered by our engineers:

- **Control system design** — process control philosophy, electrical and
  control architecture across the whole line.
- **PLC programming** — IDEC PLCs running the machine-level control:
  interlocks, sequencing, drives, drying control.
- **SCADA** — Ignition SCADA over the top: plant-wide visualisation,
  alarming, trending and records, built on high-performance HMI principles
  so an operator sees state at a glance, at 3am, mid-season.

## Commissioning method

Staged, line-section by line-section, working around production. Every
interlock proven at the panel before it was trusted from the screen. The
system was handed over with operator training and documentation, not just a
login.

## Outcomes

The plant runs its full processing line under one integrated control system,
supervised from a single Ignition SCADA. Quantified operating results are
available on request — and will be published here with the client's written
approval.
