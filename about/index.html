WHY WE DO IT

The story, briefly

It started in Cape Town, writing SCADA for jam factories and gypsum plants — and the system that secured a prison, gate by gate. The hook was simple and hasn't let go since: making real machinery do exactly what it should, reliably, when it counts.

Three decades later that idea has crossed most of heavy industry — mining, rail, water, manufacturing, agriculture — and it still decides how we engineer. Reliability isn't a spec line to us; it's a promise to whoever is depending on the plant at 3am.

Today the delivery work runs through Qualitiv: SCADA and PLC control across food and agribusiness, prototypes for people with hard ideas, and a partner network we trust on site — Corematic for machine vision, Tronlec for electrical work, and specialist process consultants where the job demands them. We do the engineering and carry the design responsibility; partners are engaged openly, never hidden behind a logo.

And the name? The hopping mouse is a small native animal that survives the harshest country in Australia by being quick, efficient and active at night. We identify with that.
