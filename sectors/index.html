SECTORS

Different plants.
Same physics.

A dryer, a pump station and a traction substation fail in different ways — but they're all real machinery that has to do exactly what it should, reliably, when it counts. Here's where we've proven that.

SECTOR / WATER & WASTEWATER

Water: networks, treatment and telemetry

A decade inside the water industry — treatment plants, sewage pump stations, wide-area radio telemetry networks — delivering and supporting the control systems utilities depend on, through to principal-level OT governance for a major South-East Queensland utility. Live brownfield upgrades on operating assets are a speciality: the sewage doesn't stop for commissioning.

ClearSCADA · Citect · MultiSmart · Kingfisher · SCADAPack · DNP3 radio telemetry

SECTOR / AGRICULTURE & FOOD

Agriculture: processing plants that run the season

Nut processing, macadamia and grain drying, seed grading, wood fibre refining — food and agribusiness is where our current delivery work is concentrated. Seasonal plants get one shot at the harvest: the control system has to hold up around the clock, be operable by a small team, and protect product quality while it does.

Ignition SCADA · IDEC PLCs · dryer control · batch & quality traceability

SECTOR / RAIL

Rail: traction power and tunnel systems

Maintained and upgraded the SCADA behind 25/50kV traction power across Queensland — three control rooms, more than two hundred RTUs and a custom DNP3 driver — plus tunnel ventilation control and a 2000-camera metropolitan CCTV network. Rail teaches a discipline other sectors borrow from us: nothing ships without a rollback plan.

Citect · Kingfisher RTUs · custom DNP3 · Wonderware · Omron / ABB / Allen-Bradley

SECTOR / MANUFACTURING & MINING

Manufacturing: machine control and condition monitoring

From polymer curing ovens on IDEC PLCs to remote condition monitoring hardware we designed from the PCB up for vibrating screens on BHP and Anglo American sites — manufacturing and mining work spanning machine control, instrumentation and the electronics behind it. When the off-the-shelf answer doesn't exist, we build it.

IDEC · Citect · bespoke instrumentation · vibration & thermal condition monitoring
