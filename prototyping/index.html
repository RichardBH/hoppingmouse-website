Ideas die in committees and slide decks. We build the proof-of-validation prototype — electronics, firmware, software, AI — so you can test the idea against reality, then bin it or back it with evidence.

PCB design and bespoke instrumentation — strain gauges, high-speed counters, analog interfaces. Hardware that survives a plant, not a bench.
FIRMWARE
